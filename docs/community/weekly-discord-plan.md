# Weekly Discord Plan

## Purpose
Keep MetaDyn's Discord active, founder-led, and visibly tied to the platform build. The goal is to create daily momentum, convert survey and beta interest into active participation, and maintain a steady rhythm of updates without going quiet.

## Core approach
Each day should include:
1. **One simple post/theme** tied to current momentum
2. **One engagement mechanic** that invites lightweight participation
3. **One ops/community task** that quietly improves follow-through

## Weekly cadence

### Tuesday — Gratitude + momentum
- **Post/theme:** Thank everyone who filled out the survey and signed up for beta. Share 2–3 specific things the community asked for.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Quick poll — "What should we show first in the beta?"
- **Ops/community task:** DM or tag beta signups with a short thank-you and note that onboarding details are coming this week.

### Wednesday — Builder signal
- **Post/theme:** Share one real build update: screenshot, GIF, or short note about a feature in progress.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Ask, "What should MetaDyn feel like in one word?"
- **Ops/community task:** Use a dedicated `#build-log` or `#beta-updates` channel consistently.

### Thursday — Beta onboarding
- **Post/theme:** Explain the beta flow clearly: who gets in first, what feedback is most useful, and what happens next.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Thread prompt — "Want early access? Drop your use case in one sentence."
- **Ops/community task:** Maintain a lightweight beta tracker: Discord handle, use case, contacted/not contacted, onboarding status.

### Friday — Community co-creation
- **Post/theme:** Ask for feedback on one specific product decision.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Emoji vote on 2–3 options.
- **Ops/community task:** Summarize the best feedback into one internal note the team can act on.

### Saturday — Social proof + vibe
- **Post/theme:** Highlight a few interesting community members, survey insights, or early tester motivations.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Ask, "What would you build first inside MetaDyn?"
- **Ops/community task:** Personally welcome any new members from the week and point them to the right channels.

### Sunday — Recap + next-week hook
- **Post/theme:** Recap what happened this week, what was learned, and what’s next.
- **Engagement mechanic:** Ask what people want more of next week: build logs, demos, polls, or beta invites.
- **Ops/community task:** Queue 3 anchor posts for next week so the Discord never goes quiet.

## Daily agenda format
Use this structure for the morning agenda message:

- **Today’s Discord focus:** <day theme>
- **Primary post:** <what to post today>
- **Engagement prompt:** <poll/question/thread>
- **Quiet ops task:** <follow-up/admin/community task>
- **If there’s extra time:** <optional stretch task>

## Default daily agenda picks by day

### Tuesday
- **Today’s Discord focus:** Gratitude + momentum
- **Primary post:** Thank survey respondents and beta signups; share 2–3 top requests
- **Engagement prompt:** Poll on what to show first in beta
- **Quiet ops task:** Reach out to beta signups with a brief thank-you
- **If there’s extra time:** Pull 3 notable survey quotes for later posting

### Wednesday
- **Today’s Discord focus:** Show visible progress
- **Primary post:** Share a screenshot/GIF/build note
- **Engagement prompt:** "What should MetaDyn feel like in one word?"
- **Quiet ops task:** Seed and organize `#build-log` / `#beta-updates`
- **If there’s extra time:** Reply to any high-signal community comments from Tuesday

### Thursday
- **Today’s Discord focus:** Beta onboarding clarity
- **Primary post:** Explain beta process and expectations
- **Engagement prompt:** Ask for one-sentence use cases in a thread
- **Quiet ops task:** Update beta tracker
- **If there’s extra time:** Identify the highest-value early users to prioritize

### Friday
- **Today’s Discord focus:** Co-creation
- **Primary post:** Present one specific decision the community can help shape
- **Engagement prompt:** Emoji vote between options
- **Quiet ops task:** Capture actionable feedback in one note
- **If there’s extra time:** Draft a short "what we learned from the community" post

### Saturday
- **Today’s Discord focus:** Community spotlight
- **Primary post:** Highlight member motivations / early tester energy
- **Engagement prompt:** "What would you build first inside MetaDyn?"
- **Quiet ops task:** Welcome new members personally
- **If there’s extra time:** Invite 1–2 strong voices to become regular contributors/testers

### Sunday
- **Today’s Discord focus:** Weekly recap + anticipation
- **Primary post:** Share recap + next-week hook
- **Engagement prompt:** Ask what kind of posts people want more of next week
- **Quiet ops task:** Queue 3 anchor posts for next week
- **If there’s extra time:** Prepare Monday’s opening build update

## Scheduling notes
- Preferred timezone for delivery: **America/Chicago**
- Morning agenda should be sent at a consistent local morning time
- Better to schedule the cron job with an explicit timezone than to depend on the whole server timezone
- If needed later, this plan can be extended into a rotating weekly program with recurring rituals (build log day, beta spotlight day, AMA day, poll day, recap day)
