# MetaDyn Opportunity Brief Framework

## Purpose
This framework is for a recurring **MetaDyn opportunity brief** focused on identifying outreach targets for immersive spaces, digital twins, experiential builds, and related platform work.

These briefs are separate from the daily agenda. They should act as a running business-development research archive.

## Schedule intent
- Run overnight on a recurring schedule
- Produce one brief per run
- Save each brief to the archive path below
- Post a concise summary to the Architects Discord channel so Josh can review examples and promising leads

## Archive structure
Preserve this structure unless Josh explicitly changes it:

- `docs/operations/opportunity-briefs/<Month-YYYY>/YYYY-MM-DD-metadyn-opportunity-brief.md`

## What each brief should do
Each brief should help answer:
- Who looks ripe for outreach right now?
- Why are they a fit for MetaDyn specifically?
- How should MetaDyn position the cold outreach?
- Which opportunities are local, national, or global?
- Which targets are small, medium, or enterprise?

## Research scope
Every brief should scan for opportunities across:
- **Local** — prioritize Kansas City area organizations and regional opportunities
- **National** — U.S. opportunities outside Kansas City
- **Global** — international opportunities where MetaDyn’s positioning is strong

Each brief should include a mix of:
- **Small business / organization**
- **Medium-sized organization**
- **Enterprise / major institution**

## Best-fit opportunity themes
Prefer targets where MetaDyn’s platform strengths are unusually relevant:
- immersive spaces and branded environments
- social / multiplayer presence
- cross-platform continuity
- portable identity and portable assets
- browser-first access with low-friction onboarding
- analytics and custom data ingestion inside immersive environments
- digital twins, training, simulation, culture, tourism, community, and experiential marketing
- open / extensible architecture instead of closed platform lock-in

## For each target, capture
- organization name
- geography (`Local`, `National`, `Global`)
- size (`Small`, `Medium`, `Enterprise`)
- sector / type
- why it looks timely or promising
- best MetaDyn angle
- recommended cold outreach positioning
- suggested next step
- confidence (`High`, `Medium`, `Low`)

## Output structure
Use this structure:

**MetaDyn Opportunity Brief — YYYY-MM-DD**
- **Why these opportunities now:**
  - 2-4 bullets summarizing what patterns make these targets ripe
- **Top 5 immediate reachouts:**
  - a fast founder skim with the 5 strongest targets
  - each should include a short one-line reason / positioning angle
- **Full top 20 opportunities:**
  - exactly 20 targets unless Josh explicitly asks for a different count
  - grouped by `Local`, `National`, and `Global`
  - every target should include the full field set:
    - organization name
    - geography
    - size
    - sector / type
    - why it looks timely or promising
    - best MetaDyn angle
    - recommended cold outreach positioning
    - suggested next step
    - confidence
    - signal/source notes when available
- **Best outreach angles:**
  - 3-6 bullets with reusable positioning language
- **Recommended immediate reachouts:**
  - ranked list of the same top 5 to contact first
- **Notes / caveats:**
  - anything uncertain, timing-sensitive, or worth validating manually

## Quality bar
- Be concrete, not generic
- Favor credible, research-backed targets over filler
- Do not overstate claims about the target’s needs
- Position MetaDyn as the best fit based on actual platform strengths, not empty hype
- Keep it readable and founder-useful

## Delivery guidance
After writing the full brief file, post the **full detailed 20-target list** to the Architects Discord channel, not just a short summary.

The Architects post should include:
- the date/title
- all 20 targets
- for each target, the same detailed field set used in the brief:
  - Geography
  - Size
  - Sector/type
  - Why timely
  - Best MetaDyn angle
  - Outreach positioning
  - Suggested next step
  - Confidence
- the saved file path

Do not collapse either the written brief or the Architects Discord post into only 3-5 targets unless Josh explicitly asks for a shorter version.
