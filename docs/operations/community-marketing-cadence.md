# Community and Marketing Cadence

Last updated: 2026-03-17

## Purpose

This document defines a simple operating rhythm for MetaDyn's near-term community and marketing work.

It is intentionally practical. The immediate goal is to:

- thank survey respondents and beta signups
- onboard current beta testers cleanly
- activate the Discord/community layer
- create visible product feedback loops
- sharpen MetaDyn's external narrative
- build credibility through proof-heavy content and targeted outreach

## Current Strategic Framing

Use this working external line consistently:

**MetaDyn builds immersive digital infrastructure for the next internet.**

Support it with three proof pillars:

- cross-platform immersive delivery
- ownership of identity, presence, and assets
- operator experience building beyond the limitations of existing immersive platforms

## Immediate Priorities

1. **Thank-you and beta outreach**
   - Contact all survey respondents and beta signups quickly.
   - Use a short message structure:
     - thank them
     - confirm fit/use case
     - give one next step

2. **Beta onboarding**
   - Create one clear onboarding path.
   - Every tester should know:
     - what MetaDyn is
     - what stage the product is at
     - what to do first
     - where to report feedback

3. **Community activation**
   - Make Discord feel active and purposeful.
   - Seed conversation with narrow prompts, progress updates, clips, screenshots, and direct asks.

4. **Feedback visibility**
   - Summarize bugs, friction, feature requests, and repeated questions weekly.
   - Show the loop publicly with a lightweight "you said / we're doing" recap.

5. **Credibility-building content**
   - Publish depth and proof, not vague hype.
   - Focus on product progress, architecture clarity, operator lessons, and why current platforms fall short.

## Weekly Cadence

### Monday — planning and segmentation

- Review:
  - new survey responses
  - new beta signups
  - Discord joins
  - prior-week feedback
- Tag people into simple buckets:
  - `beta-ready`
  - `warm community`
  - `partners`
  - `inactive`
- Choose one narrative theme for the week:
  - identity
  - presence
  - digital twins
  - immersive commerce
  - builder tooling
  - cross-platform continuity
- Set three weekly outputs:
  - 1 credibility post
  - 1 outreach batch
  - 1 community activation post

### Tuesday — beta onboarding

- Send one batch onboarding/welcome message to all current beta-ready contacts.
- Point everyone to one primary onboarding location and one primary feedback location.
- Ask each tester to complete exactly one first action:
  - log in
  - test one flow
  - join one Discord channel/thread
  - submit one feedback note
- Track status simply:
  - invited
  - replied
  - onboarded
  - active
  - stalled

### Wednesday — outreach and relationship building

- Send thank-you outreach to:
  - survey respondents
  - beta signups
  - especially engaged early supporters
- Lightly personalize the opening line.
- Follow up with non-responders.
- Update pipeline notes by segment:
  - brands
  - enterprise
  - creators
  - builders / technical partners

### Thursday — content and community activation

- Publish one proof-based asset:
  - founder/operator point of view
  - technical explainer
  - product progress note
  - demo clip
  - architecture/narrative post
- Repurpose it into:
  - Discord copy
  - outreach snippets
  - short-form social copy
- In Discord, post:
  - one discussion prompt
  - one progress artifact
  - one direct ask for reactions or opinions

### Friday — feedback and refinement

- Collect and summarize:
  - bugs
  - friction points
  - feature requests
  - repeated confusion
- Publish a short "you said / we're doing" recap.
- Review:
  - replies
  - calls booked
  - testers activated
  - strongest narrative angle
  - recurring objections
- Adjust next week's theme and CTA based on what actually got response.

### Weekend — light-touch presence

- Keep it low pressure:
  - one poll
  - one screenshot
  - one member highlight
  - one casual prompt

## Monthly Cadence

### Week 1 — messaging refresh

- Tighten the shared external story across:
  - site copy
  - one-pagers
  - outreach language
  - docs where appropriate
- Reconfirm:
  - why now
  - why MetaDyn
  - why not existing platforms

### Week 2 — beta and pipeline push

- Run one focused push around one ICP at a time:
  - enterprise / digital twins
  - brands / immersive activations
  - creators / builders
- Prioritize design-partner style testers and high-signal prospects.

### Week 3 — authority asset

- Publish one larger credibility piece:
  - thesis post
  - product vision memo
  - simplified architecture overview
  - state-of-the-market perspective

### Week 4 — review and pruning

- Review:
  - lead sources
  - reply rates
  - onboarding conversion
  - active testers
  - strongest use cases
  - top objections and confusion points
- Prune weak narratives.
- Double down on the strongest message and segment.

## Community Operating Structure

Discord should at minimum support:

- welcome
- beta updates
- feedback
- general/showcase

Guidelines:

- seed activity instead of waiting for it
- make every call-to-action singular and simple
- recognize useful early contributors visibly
- optimize for activation, not audience size

## Marketing Operating Structure

Weekly minimum targets:

- 10–20 targeted outbound touches
- 3–5 follow-ups
- 1 credibility asset
- 1 founder-led narrative piece
- 1 beta/community touchpoint or demo block

Guidelines:

- treat every content asset as both market education and sales enablement
- lead with practical outcomes, not abstract metaverse language alone
- repeat one clear story consistently before expanding into more campaigns

## Narrative Spine

Use this sequence when building messaging:

1. current internet experiences are fragmented and disposable
2. immersive experiences need continuity, identity, and ownership
3. existing platforms limit control and long-term value
4. MetaDyn provides both the connective layer and the immersive spaces
5. therefore MetaDyn is not just building worlds; it is building infrastructure
