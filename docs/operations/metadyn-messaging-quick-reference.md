## Messaging rules

### Always do
- lead with clarity
- make it obvious MetaDyn is infrastructure, not just content
- connect the message to identity, presence, continuity, or ownership
- keep the wording aligned across channels

### Avoid
- vague hype
- inconsistent descriptions from post to post
- sounding like just another world-building studio
- making MetaDyn sound like only a Discord community
