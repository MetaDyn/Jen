# MetaDyn Opportunity Brief — 2026-04-09

- **Why these opportunities now:**
  - Multiple major immersive openings and expansions are landing in 2026, which creates a familiar pre-launch problem: institutions can build a one-off attraction, but they still need continuity across web, venue, identity, audience retention, and repeat programming.
  - Kansas City has unusually strong near-term local timing because FIFA World Cup 26 activity, Union Station programming, and the broader regional appetite for immersive experiences create a live market for sponsor, visitor, and hybrid digital/physical activations.
  - Museum, tourism, and experiential operators are clearly investing in AV-heavy immersive builds; MetaDyn’s opening is not “bigger projections,” but the layer those builds often lack: browser-first access, social presence, portable identity/assets, and analytics-rich persistent worlds.
  - The strongest cold targets are the ones already signaling expansion, new openings, or experimentation with interactive storytelling, digital arts, tourism, or digital twin thinking.

- **Top opportunities:**
