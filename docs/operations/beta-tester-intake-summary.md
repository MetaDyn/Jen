## Repeated pain-point themes
- Browser-first access / avoiding mandatory app installs
- Better visual quality and rendering fidelity
- Higher concurrency and better scale
- Embeddable immersive spaces inside websites
- Self-hosting, custom URLs, and stronger control/security
- Distrust of platforms that shift toward gatekeeping after communities invest heavily
- Community interconnection across spaces
