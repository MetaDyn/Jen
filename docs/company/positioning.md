# MetaDyn Positioning

## 1. Canonical Internal Definition

**MetaDyn is building the future of the internet: a next-generation digital fabric that connects people, places, and things through immersive experiences, with true ownership of identity, presence, and 3D assets.**

This is the strongest high-level internal framing because it captures:
- MetaDyn's scale of ambition
- the idea of a connective digital fabric
- immersive experience as a native medium
- ownership of identity, presence, and 3D assets as a core differentiator

## 2. Public-Facing Short Version

**MetaDyn builds immersive digital infrastructure for the next internet — connecting people, places, and things with true ownership of identity, presence, and 3D assets.**

This version keeps the core idea but is slightly more grounded and externally legible.

## 3. Expanded Mission Statement

**MetaDyn is building the future of the internet itself: a next-generation digital fabric that connects people, places, and things in immersive ways. We create both the connective platform layer and the immersive spaces that run on it, enabling brands, enterprises, and creators to participate in a more open, persistent, and ownership-driven digital world. At the center of that vision is true ownership of identity, presence, and 3D assets.**

## Supporting Themes

The positioning above is strengthened by several truths already established elsewhere in the canonical model:
- MetaDyn is both a company and a builder community
- MetaDyn is building a next-generation alternative to limitations encountered in existing platforms
- MetaDyn serves brands, enterprises, and creators
- MetaDyn is focused not just on spaces, but on the connective fabric between platforms, systems, and experiences

## Notes

These statements are intended as the current canonical starting point. They can later be refined into:
- website copy
- investor/partner messaging
- social/community bios
- pitch decks
- documentation front matter

A current website/product-IA direction reported on 2026-05-25 is to give the platform its own clearer public surface under routes such as `platform.metadyn.xyz`, including onboarding paths like `platform.metadyn.xyz/getting-started`, while removing Photon as the active platform story from the site.
