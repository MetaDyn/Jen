# MetaDyn

## Identity

**MetaDyn** is short for **Metaverse Dynamix**.

MetaDyn is both:
- **MetaDyn, LLC**, a company registered in **Missouri, United States**
- a **vibrant community of builders** with an open-source mindset, centered primarily around Discord

## What MetaDyn Does

MetaDyn is a **metaverse builder**.

It builds:
- the **fabric that connects platforms**
- and the **immersive spaces themselves** across multiple platforms

Its work is aimed at:
- brands
- enterprises
- creators

## Strategic Context

MetaDyn brings more than **20 years of cumulative experience** in related digital/immersive work, with especially concentrated metaverse-platform work over the last **3 years**.

A major recent chapter of that work involved building on platforms such as **Spatial.io** and its Unity toolkit.

## Why MetaDyn Is Building Its Own Alternative

Existing platforms like Spatial helped validate important parts of the market, but they are no longer a strong fit for MetaDyn or its clients.

MetaDyn is therefore building a **next-generation alternative** that provides more control, flexibility, continuity, and long-term value.

## High-Level Framing

MetaDyn should be understood as a hybrid of:
- a **company**
- a **platform builder**
- a **builder community**

That matters because MetaDyn is not simply producing isolated worlds. It is working toward a broader platform layer for immersive identity, connected presence, cross-platform continuity, and deployable metaverse infrastructure.

## Canonical Positioning Draft

**MetaDyn is building the future of the internet: a next-generation digital fabric that connects people, places, and things through immersive experiences, with true ownership of identity, presence, and 3D assets.**
