## 1) Company Basics

- **Company name:** MetaDyn, LLC
- **MetaDyn means:** Metaverse Dynamix
- **Jurisdiction:** Missouri, United States
- **Core description:** MetaDyn is a metaverse builder creating both the connective layer across platforms and immersive spaces for brands, enterprises, and creators.
- **Community shape:** MetaDyn is also a builder community with an open-source-oriented mindset centered primarily around Discord.

## 2) Investor-Facing Positioning

### Primary positioning
MetaDyn builds immersive digital infrastructure for the next internet — connecting people, places, and things with true ownership of identity, presence, and 3D assets.

### Expanded positioning
MetaDyn is building a next-generation digital fabric that links identity, presence, immersive environments, and deployable metaverse infrastructure. We create both the connective platform layer and the immersive spaces that run on it, enabling brands, enterprises, and creators to participate in a more open, persistent, and ownership-driven digital world.

## 3) Problem / Market Thesis

Working draft:
- Existing immersive platforms validated the market but often limit control, continuity, portability, and long-term value capture.
- Brands, enterprises, and creators need more than isolated virtual spaces; they need infrastructure for identity continuity, persistent presence, deployable environments, and ownership of digital assets.
- MetaDyn is building a more flexible and ownership-aligned alternative.

## 4) Product / Platform Thesis

Working draft:
- MetaDyn combines a platform layer with immersive space delivery.
- Current ecosystem direction includes Unity WebGL, ThreeJS, and Hyperfy-adjacent delivery paths.
- The platform direction emphasizes identity continuity, presence, memory, orchestration, and immersive deployment.
- The Unity SDK is intended to be open source, with optional ecosystem-connected value layered on top.
- A key trust principle is that creators should be able to self-host and keep experiences alive even if they do not use MetaDyn-hosted services.
