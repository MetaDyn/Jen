# MetaDyn: Immersive Enterprise Experiences, Built Around Your Needs

## People, AI, and Spatial Context—Together

MetaDyn brings immersive environments and AI interaction together to help organizations explain complex systems, train people, and collaborate around real-world places, equipment, and processes.

Rather than separating a 3D experience from the people and information that make it useful, MetaDyn combines shared spaces, voice interaction, and contextual AI assistance in a browser-accessible experience.

## What Makes MetaDyn Different

**More than a virtual meeting space.**
Our Unity-based platform supports interactive environments built around the task at hand—from exploring a facility to understanding equipment or rehearsing a procedure.

**AI within the experience.**
Voice-enabled, embodied AI can provide a more natural way to ask questions and receive guidance within an immersive environment. The goal is assistance connected to what people are exploring—not simply a separate chat window.

**Deployment choice and long-term control.**
MetaDyn supports self-hosting of immersive experiences, with an open SDK strategy centered on customization and continuity. Organizations can discuss deployment options that fit their infrastructure needs rather than assuming every experience must live on a single shared public platform.

## A Practical Approach to Enterprise Security

Enterprise environments can contain sensitive knowledge, facility information, and operational data. We treat the handling of that information as part of the deployment discussion—not an afterthought.

Our approach is straightforward:

- **Make data boundaries clear.** Identify where information is processed and stored, which external services are involved, and what remains within customer-controlled infrastructure. Self-hosting the experience does not automatically make every connected service private.
- **Define access around the use case.** Establish who should access an experience, its information, and its administrative functions, then validate the required controls for the agreed deployment.
- **Keep AI permissions explicit.** Define what information an assistant may use and what actions it may take. Providing guidance should not automatically grant authority to change enterprise systems.
- **Start with limited exposure.** Begin evaluations with non-sensitive content and a defined workflow before considering sensitive data or production integrations.
- **Be clear about capabilities and responsibilities.** Explain what is available, what requires additional work, and who is responsible for operating and supporting the deployment.

These principles give customers concrete questions to evaluate with us. Security requirements and controls are established for each deployment; we do not treat immersive technology or self-hosting alone as a guarantee of security or compliance.

## Where Digital Twins and Immersive Technology Can Help

A shared representation of a facility, asset, or process can make complex information easier to understand and discuss. Potential applications include:

- **Training and onboarding:** explore an environment and learn procedures with contextual guidance.
- **Security preparedness:** rehearse scenarios, escalation procedures, and coordinated responses without affecting live operations.
- **Collaborative understanding:** bring distributed teams into the same spatial context to discuss equipment, locations, and processes.

A connected digital twin adds further requirements for data integration, accuracy, and freshness. We distinguish an immersive model or training simulation from a live operational system and scope those integrations accordingly.

### Proposed Upgrade Process

1. Inventory the client's current Netlify account/plan, site ownership, deployment workflow, and external service dependencies.
2. Identify the enterprise requirement driving an upgrade: governance, support, contractual availability, connectivity, or delivery protection.
3. Confirm available features, commercial arrangements, and account/site transition steps with the Netlify partner contact; do not assume a frictionless or automatic upgrade.
4. Map controls to owners across Netlify, MetaDyn, the customer, and other service providers.
5. Validate the selected configuration and provide a scoped evidence pack before expanding sensitive use.

This is a hosting progression to define, not a claim that Enterprise features are already enabled for existing clients. Netlify-managed hosting remains distinct from deploying the full stack inside customer infrastructure.
