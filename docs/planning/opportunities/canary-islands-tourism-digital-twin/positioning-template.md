# Positioning Template — Canary Islands Tourism Digital Twin Opportunity

## Core positioning
MetaDyn should be positioned as the **immersive access, interaction, and continuity layer around the digital twin**.

## Strong one-line statements
- MetaDyn helps make destination intelligence explorable, guided, and usable by humans.
- The digital twin should not just simulate the destination; it should become an accessible, social interface to the destination.
- MetaDyn can connect public engagement, stakeholder planning, and partner participation in one immersive layer.

## Best-fit value propositions
- **Browser-first access:** lowers friction for public and stakeholder adoption
- **AI avatars:** make the system guided, conversational, and easier to use
- **Social presence:** supports shared exploration, events, and collaboration
- **Analytics + custom data ingestion:** connects immersive use to measurable value
- **Extensible/open architecture:** reduces dependence on closed, one-off platform solutions

## Best stakeholder angles

### For public-sector / tourism boards
- make digital twin insights more accessible
- support destination storytelling and public engagement
- create stakeholder workshop environments
- connect planning and visitor-facing layers

### For universities / labs
- support training, research walkthroughs, and scenario interpretation
- create educational interfaces around complex destination data
- extend the usefulness of research outputs beyond technical teams

### For local businesses and partners
- create visible participation pathways
- make the ecosystem easier to understand and join
- help partners see practical engagement and discovery value

## Suggested avatar roles
- Visitor Concierge
- Local Culture Guide
- Destination Operations Analyst
- Business Participation Advisor

## Objections and responses

### Objection: “We already have digital twin tooling.”
**Response:** MetaDyn is not a replacement for the underlying planning or analytics stack. It complements that stack by making it more explorable, guided, and useful to broader stakeholder groups.

### Objection: “This sounds like a metaverse pitch.”
**Response:** The strongest use case here is practical: immersive access, stakeholder education, guided destination interaction, and public-facing usability around a complex planning initiative.

### Objection: “We don’t want a one-off gimmick.”
**Response:** MetaDyn’s value is in supporting a persistent, extensible, browser-accessible layer that can evolve over time instead of remaining a single campaign activation.

## Best next-step ask
Ask for a short exploratory conversation and offer a concise concept note or pilot framing, not a giant platform pitch.
