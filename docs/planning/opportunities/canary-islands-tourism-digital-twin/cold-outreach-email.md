# Cold Outreach Email — Canary Islands Tourism Digital Twin

## Draft 1

**Subject:** Idea for an immersive experience layer around the Canary Islands tourism digital twin work

Hello,

I’ve been following the direction of the Canary Islands tourism digital twin initiative with a lot of interest, particularly the work around AI-driven analytics, immersive simulation, visitor-flow modeling, and destination intelligence.

One of the strongest opportunities in initiatives like this is not only building the twin itself, but making it more usable and meaningful for the people who need to interact with it — including visitors, planners, local businesses, educators, and destination stakeholders.

That is where MetaDyn may be a strong fit.

MetaDyn is an immersive platform focused on browser-first access, AI avatars, social presence, analytics, and extensible cross-platform experiences. Rather than positioning ourselves as the raw digital twin backend, we see a strong opportunity to serve as the **immersive access and interaction layer around the twin**.

In practical terms, that could include things like:

- AI guide avatars for visitors and destination storytelling
- stakeholder walkthrough environments for tourism operators and planners
- immersive business participation layers for local partners
- public-facing destination experiences that connect planning intelligence with visitor engagement

We believe there is a meaningful opportunity to help make destination intelligence more explorable, guided, and usable across multiple stakeholder groups.

If this is relevant to the work underway, I’d be glad to share a short concept note showing how MetaDyn could support a tourism digital twin initiative like this in a practical and complementary way.

Best regards,

Josh Garrett  
MetaDyn
