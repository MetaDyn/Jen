## Key Principles

1. **Members First** - Community needs > individual egos
2. **Transparency** - Communicate openly and honestly
3. **Consistency** - Fair and predictable moderation
4. **Empowerment** - Give members ownership and voice
5. **Prevention > Punishment** - Design out toxicity
6. **Celebrate Wins** - Recognize positive contributions
7. **Continuous Improvement** - Listen and adapt

## Community Lifecycle Stages

### Stage 1: Foundation (0-100 members)
**Focus:** Culture setting, core values, early norms
- Manually welcome every member
- High-touch engagement
- Founder/team very active
- Set the tone for future culture

### Stage 2: Growth (100-1000 members)
**Focus:** Scaling moderation, community-led initiatives
- Recruit volunteer moderators
- Establish regular events
- Create creator programs
- Formalize processes

### Stage 3: Maturity (1000+ members)
**Focus:** Self-sustaining, community ownership
- Community-run events
- Automated moderation
- Ambassador programs
- Sub-communities and niches

## Moderation Framework

### Prevention (Best Approach)
- Clear rules and expectations
- Positive culture and role models
- Good onboarding experience
- Community accountability

### Detection
- Automated filters (profanity, spam)
- User reports
- Moderator observation
- Pattern analysis

### Response
- **Minor:** Verbal warning, education
- **Moderate:** Mute, temporary timeout
- **Severe:** Kick, temporary ban
- **Extreme:** Permanent ban, IP ban

### Restoration
- Appeal process for bans
- Path to redemption
- Second chances with conditions
- Community reintegration

## Crisis Response Playbook

### When Bad Things Happen
1. **Acknowledge** - "We're aware, investigating"
2. **Assess** - Severity, scope, responsible parties
3. **Act** - Immediate moderation action
4. **Communicate** - Transparent update to community
5. **Support** - Help affected members
6. **Reflect** - What went wrong, how to prevent
7. **Rebuild** - Restore trust, improve systems
